## 🖤 About The Game

**No Gods Ahead** takes place in a shattered, post-apocalyptic Poland following the detonation of an experimental bomb that didn't just destroy the land—**it destroyed time itself.**

You play as a soldier trapped inside an enemy facility. Unaware that the explosion even occurred, you break free, only to step out into a broken world where space-time has been fractured:

* **🛑 Frozen Time:** Certain zones are forever trapped at the exact millisecond of the blast.
* **⏪ Rewound Past:** Areas restored to their state moments prior to the detonation.
* **⏩ Ruined Future:** Wastelands suffering the aftermath decades later.

Players must master unique environmental time-manipulation mechanics to navigate different regions of the country, overcome anomalies, and uncover the mystery behind the weapon's origin.

## 🛠️ Technical Overview

This project was built from the ground up as a hands-on dive into **C++ game programming**, engine architecture, and environmental storytelling in Unreal Engine.

* **Engine:** Unreal Engine 4.27.2
* **Core Logic:** Hybrid system combining **C++** (for performance-critical logic/systems) and **Blueprints** (for rapid prototyping)
* **Team Structure:**
  * **1x Game Developer** (C++ Architecture, Mechanics & Logic)
  * **1x Game Designer** (Level Design, Asset Creation & World Building)
* **Assets & Pipeline:** Custom 3D assets created in **Blender** and refined within Unreal Engine.
* **Quality Assurance:** Tested by a dedicated group of **10+ playtesters** after each major release to gather feedback and balance core gameplay mechanics.
